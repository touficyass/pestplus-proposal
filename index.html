3 AI Sales Chatbot

A sales agent that never sleeps

  • Answers instantly about services, safety, coverage areas and preparation, in English and Arabic.
  • Qualifies leads: asks property type, pest, size and urgency, then gives a price range.
  • Books the visit directly into the CRM calendar and confirms by WhatsApp.
  • Upsells smartly: suggests cleaning with pest control, and contracts after one-off jobs.
  • Hands over to a human with full context when needed.

Business impact

No missed inquiry at night or on weekends, faster response than any competitor, less load on the call team, and higher conversion because customers are answered while their interest is highest.
